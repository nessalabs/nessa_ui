## react-resizable-panels

Portions of `packages/react/src/components/split-view/split-view-math.ts`
are adapted from
[react-resizable-panels](https://github.com/bvaughn/react-resizable-panels)
at commit
[`f9c422714a66e14f671a17f340a3560d8032fcdc`](https://github.com/bvaughn/react-resizable-panels/tree/f9c422714a66e14f671a17f340a3560d8032fcdc)
(version 4.12.3).

Adapted functions: `adjustLayoutByDelta`, `validatePanelGroupLayout`,
`validatePanelSize`, `calculateDefaultLayout`,
`calculateSeparatorAriaValues`, and the layout-number tolerance helpers.
The adaptations remove the upstream library's global mutable state,
disabled-panel handling, and non-px/% units; the algorithms are otherwise
preserved. No upstream code is redistributed unmodified, and
react-resizable-panels is not a runtime dependency of this project.
